## Purpose of This Document

This file declares how Artificial Intelligence (AI) tools were used during the
development of **Stowaway**. It is intended to maintain transparency and honesty
regarding the role of AI in our project, in line with the rules and spirit of
TatHack '26.

---

## AI Tool Used

| Tool | Model | Effort Level |
|------|-------|--------------|
| Claude (Free version) | Sonnet 5.5 | Medium |

Only **Claude Sonnet 5.5 (Medium effort)** on the **free plan** was used during
this project. No other AI tools were used.

---

## How We Used AI

AI was used as a **supportive aid** throughout the project — never to replace our
own work. Specifically, Claude helped us in the following ways:

### 1. Connecting Our Ideas
Claude was used to help us **connect and structure our ideas**. We came up with
the core concept of Stowaway ourselves, and Claude helped us see how the
different pieces — encryption, steganography, and error correction — could fit
together into a coherent solution.

### 2. Learning the Concepts
Claude was used as a **learning tool** to understand the concepts required for
our project, including:

- Reed–Solomon error correction
- Single Event Upsets (SEUs) and radiation effects on electronics
- Steganographic techniques in images
- Encryption fundamentals

This helped us build the project with genuine understanding, not just copied code.

### 3. Correcting & Debugging Errors
Claude was used to help us **identify, correct, and debug errors** in our code.
All fixes were reviewed, understood, and applied by the team — not blindly copied.

### 4. Formatting
Claude was used for a few **formatting-related tasks**, such as:

- Structuring documentation
- Formatting the project description and YouTube description
- Cleaning up this declaration file

---

## What AI Was NOT Used For

- ❌ Generating the entire project or its core logic.
- ❌ Making final design or architecture decisions.
- ❌ Creating deliverables without review or modification by the team.
- ❌ Any use that would violate the originality or fairness rules of the hackathon.
